# Buying Journey Enablement Example: Industrial

**Offering:** NFA-500 retrofit controller (Northfield Automation)
**Motions:** direct to large plants; 8 partners (distributors and one integrator)
**Evidence:** win/loss on 8 lost deals; interviews with 3 partners

**Why this example exists:** the group is larger, the specification is often written by an
outside integrator, and partners do most of the selling. The gap sits at **requirements
building**, where the spec is written around a competitor.

---

```markdown
## Journey map

| Buying job | Roles active | Needs to move forward | Stall (checked / hypothesis) | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem identification | Maintenance engineer, plant manager | See the cost of the aging controller | Hypothesis | Status-quo checklist (build) |
| Solution exploration | Maintenance engineer, integrator | Learn that a shift-long retrofit exists | 3 of 8: never considered retrofit | Application note, partner explainer |
| Requirements | **Integrator**, technical evaluator | Spec text and standards summary | **5 of 8 lost: spec named a competitor** | **Spec text + standards summary (missing)** |
| Supplier selection | Procurement, plant manager | Comparable offer, TCO | 2 of 8: unlike-for-like comparison | TCO sheet, battle card |
| Validation | Maintenance engineer | Reference at a similar plant | 3 of 8: no reference in their industry | Reference case (one, permission pending) |
| Consensus | Plant manager, finance | Payback and shutdown risk | Hypothesis | Business-case template (build) |

## Direct vs partner
Distributors: short sales guide, demo unit access, bracket-kit stocking plan, margin sheet.
Integrators: spec text, I/O mapping guide, program import tool, early access to firmware. Direct
team: full narrative, site survey process, battle cards.

## Training and certification
Sales level (half a day: narrative, top objections). Technical level (two days: configure,
install with the bracket kit, commission; **tested on a demo rig**). Service level for partners who
commission. Certified partners are listed, get lead sharing, and a demo unit. Recertify at each
major firmware release.

## Feedback loop
Partner managers log objections and hotline questions; monthly review for six months with Sales,
Service, and product; assets updated; a short "what changed" note to partners.

## Missing assets (ranked)
1. Spec text and standards summary for integrators (5 of 8 lost deals)
2. Business-case template for the plant manager and finance
3. Second and third reference cases (permission needed)

## Stall hypotheses to check
1. Is "no reference in their industry" a real blocker or an excuse? Call two lost accounts.
```

## What to learn from this

- **The biggest gap is a role the team doesn't sell to.** The integrator wrote the spec; no asset
  served them.
- **Partners get a different toolkit, not a shorter one.** Distributors need stocking and margin;
  integrators need spec text.
- **Certification is tested on a demo rig.** Attendance alone doesn't commission a controller.
