# Altitude & Horizon Template

Two worksheets: the **Cascading Context Map** (the artifact you hand your team) and the
**Transition Self-Assessment** (the private one you keep). Quality checks at the bottom.

## Provenance
Based on The Product Porch
[E42 — From Product Manager to Director](https://the-product-porch-43ca35c0.simplecast.com/episodes/from-product-manager-to-director-how-to-make-the-shift-part-1).

---

## Worksheet 1: Cascading Context Map

The artifact. One page beats ten — the goal is clarity, not comprehensiveness.

```markdown
## Context Cascade: [Team or Portfolio] — [Quarter]

**Company Priority:** [What leadership actually said, in their words. Quote it.]

**Business Unit Translation:** [How your BU contributes to that priority]

**Product Portfolio Translation:** [How your products contribute to that]

**Team Accountabilities:**
- [Team or person]: [what they own specifically]
- [Team or person]: [what they own specifically]

**Why this matters:** [The so-what. What changes, and what deliberately stays the same.]

**What I'm still unsure about:** [Name the gaps rather than papering over them.
Your team already suspects where the ambiguity is.]
```

**Repeat one block per company priority.** Three to five priorities, not twenty bullets — if you
can't cascade it, it wasn't a priority.

## Worksheet 2: Transition Self-Assessment

Private. Diagnosis before prescription — name the zone before you try to fix the friction.

```markdown
## Altitude & Horizon Self-Assessment — [Date]

### Where I'm operating today

**Altitude:** [Feature/team level -> portfolio/org level] — where am I actually spending time?
**Horizon:** [Sprint-to-quarter -> quarter-to-annual] — how far ahead am I actually looking?

Evidence, not aspiration: [what last week's calendar says about both]

### The four transition zones

| Zone | How I'm doing | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. [Zone name] | [Strong / Developing / Weak] | [specific recent example] |
| 2. | | |
| 3. | | |
| 4. | | |

**Weakest zone:** [which one, and what it's costing]

### Waiter or operator?

- Last three times something broke, did I solve it or coach someone through it? [answer]
- What am I still doing that someone on my team could own? [list]
- What am I NOT doing that only I can do? [list]

### Hero Syndrome triggers

- I feel the pull to jump in when: [specific situations]
- The cost when I do: [what doesn't get done at director altitude]

### One change this month
[A single behavioral commitment, specific enough to check in 30 days]
```

## Quality checks

**The cascade**
- [ ] The company priority is quoted, not paraphrased into something tidier
- [ ] Three to five priorities, not a wall of bullets
- [ ] Every layer connects upward — a reader can trace team work to company strategy
- [ ] Team accountabilities name who owns what, not just what exists
- [ ] The "so-what" says what **stays the same**, not only what changes
- [ ] Gaps are named. Manufacturing false certainty costs more than admitting ambiguity
- [ ] It fits on one page

**The honest test**
- [ ] You wrote it without waiting for perfect clarity from above. Waiting is the PM habit;
      creating imperfect-but-useful clarity is the Director skill
- [ ] A team member could read it and answer "why am I working on this?" unprompted

**The self-assessment**
- [ ] Altitude and horizon are answered from your calendar, not your intentions
- [ ] Each zone rating cites a specific recent example
- [ ] The waiter/operator questions got uncomfortable answers — if none did, you rated yourself,
      not your behavior
- [ ] The one change is checkable in 30 days
