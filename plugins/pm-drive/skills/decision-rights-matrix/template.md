## Quality checks
- [ ] Each decision has exactly one accountable person
- [ ] "Consulted" is defined (heard, not agreement)
- [ ] Vetoes are rare and carry a stated reason
- [ ] Forums state what they decide and what they don't
- [ ] Escalation has a name and a time limit
- [ ] The named people confirmed; leadership endorsed
- [ ] Only the decisions that cause friction are mapped
