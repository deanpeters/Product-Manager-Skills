# EOL Internal Enablement Template

Pick a level, pin the two dates, then build only the sections your level warrants. Quality checks at
the bottom.

## Provenance
Adapted from `prompts/eol-internal-enablement.md` in the
`https://github.com/deanpeters/product-manager-prompts` repo.

---

## Pick your level first

| | Level 1 — Light | Level 2 — Standard | Level 3 — Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Feature, internal tool, API | Commercial product, active customers | Revenue-critical, hardware, regulated |
| Produces | Support FAQ only | + Sales talking points, objections, escalation | + Channel brief, account tiers, training |
| Prep time | An afternoon | A week | Several weeks with live training |

**Most packs are Level 2.** Never default to the heaviest.

**Two dates, always separated:** enablement complete → *then* announcement.

**Sticky-note rule:** bullets are 4-8 words; FAQ answers are 1-2 sentences.

### 1. Support FAQ (all levels)

Organized by what customers ask, highest call volume first.

#### What is happening?
- Q: [Customer question in plain language]
- A: [Honest, specific answer in 1-2 sentences]

#### What does this mean for me?
- Q: [Impact question]
- A: [What continues, what changes]

#### What are my options?
- Q: [Migration or alternative question]
- A: [Concrete next step with timeline]

#### What about my data?
- Q: [Access, export, retention question]
- A: [Format, deadline, retention window]

#### What about my contract?
- Q: [Contractual question]
- A: [Answer aligned with Legal guidance]

#### When does support end?
- Q: [Support timeline question]
- A: [Specific dates per phase]

#### Escalation path
- Level 1: [Name/role -- standard questions]
- Level 2: [Name/role -- unhappy customers]
- Level 3: [Name/role -- churn-risk accounts]
- Executive: [Name/role -- named accounts, press, legal]

### 3. Objection Handling (L2+)

Acknowledge -- Reframe -- Offer. Every offer pre-approved.

#### "We just bought this / renewed last quarter."
- Acknowledge: [validate the frustration]
- Reframe: [honest reason or genuine upside]
- Offer: [approved accommodation]

#### "The replacement doesn't have feature X."
- Acknowledge: [validate the gap]
- Reframe: [what it does better]
- Offer: [workaround or parity timeline]

#### "We're going to leave entirely."
- Acknowledge: [take it seriously]
- Reframe: [what staying preserves]
- Offer: [retention path or exec conversation]

#### "Why should we trust your next product?"
- Acknowledge: [validate the trust concern]
- Reframe: [what this transition demonstrates]
- Offer: [commitment or continuity guarantee]

#### "Can you make an exception for us?"
- Acknowledge: [validate the ask]
- Reframe: [what is and isn't flexible]
- Offer: [the real accommodation available]

(Add 2-3 objections specific to this product.)

## Quality checks

Any "no" is a rewrite, not a nitpick.

**Timing**
- [ ] Enablement-complete date precedes the announcement date on the plan
- [ ] Support sees this before customers do — not the same morning

**FAQ**
- [ ] Organized by what customers ask, not internal categories
- [ ] Highest-call-volume questions come first
- [ ] Every answer is 1-2 sentences and specific — no "we're evaluating options"
- [ ] Every answer is actually true (if it isn't, the plan is the problem, not the wording)

**Comparison table**
- [ ] At least one honest gap is named, with workaround or parity date
- [ ] A rep could hand this to a customer without being embarrassed

**Objections**
- [ ] Every response acknowledges before reframing
- [ ] No response tells the customer their reaction is wrong
- [ ] **Every offer is pre-approved with a limit** — nothing ends in "I'll see what I can do"
- [ ] The five predictable objections are covered, plus 2-3 product-specific ones

**Escalation**
- [ ] Four rungs, each with a name — not a role that maps to nobody
- [ ] Each rung has a trigger a rep can recognize mid-call
- [ ] A rep could answer "who do I call at 4pm Friday?" from this

**Proportion**
- [ ] Level was chosen deliberately, not defaulted to the heaviest
- [ ] If you went lighter, you know which material dropped out
