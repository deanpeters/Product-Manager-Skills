# Buying Journey Enablement Example: SaaS

**Offering:** Route Optimizer (Fieldlight add-on)
**Motions:** direct sales to existing customers; resellers
**Evidence:** win/loss on 10 recent deals

**Why this example exists:** most of the toolkit is built for the user and the technical
evaluator. The journey map shows deals dying at **consensus creation**, where finance appears
with no asset.

---

```markdown
## Journey map

| Buying job | Roles active | Needs to move forward | Stall (checked against 10 deals) | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem identification | Dispatcher, ops manager | See the cost of rebuilding the day | 4 of 10: "it works fine" | Cost calculator (build) |
| Solution exploration | Ops manager | Know what kinds of scheduling help exist | Not seen as a stall | Explainer video (exists) |
| Requirements | Ops manager, IT | Security and data answers | 2 of 10: IT asked about location data late | Security summary (DPA addendum in review) |
| Supplier selection | Ops manager, procurement | Comparable offer, clear price | Not seen | Proposal template (exists) |
| Validation | Dispatcher, ops manager | See it run on their own day | 5 of 10: no trial data | Sandbox trial guide (exists) |
| Consensus creation | **CFO**, sponsor | A number they can defend | **7 of 10 stalled here: finance had nothing** | **Business-case template (missing)** |

## Asset inventory (excerpt)
| Asset | Stage | Role | Channel | Owner | As-of | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product feature deck | none | none | Direct | PMM | stale | Delete |
| Trial guide | Validation | Dispatcher | Direct, reseller | CS | Jun | Keep |
| Business-case template | Consensus | CFO | Direct, reseller | `[NEEDS SOURCE]` | n/a | **Build first** |

## Direct vs partner
Resellers get a 5-minute demo script and the one-page value summary, not the 40-slide deck, plus
margin on the add-on. They do not get the technical security deep dive; they escalate to a named
engineer.

## Training and certification
Sales (30 min, narrative and top four objections); Technical (setup and first optimization in the
sandbox, tested by doing it). Resellers who complete both are listed as certified and get lead
sharing.

## Feedback loop
CS and reseller managers log objections in the CRM; monthly 30-minute review for six months; cards
and assets updated; a "what changed" note to reps and resellers.

## Stall hypotheses to check
1. Do CFOs stall because they lack a number, or because they see the add-on as optional? Check with
   three lost deals.
```

## What to learn from this

- **Seven of ten deals stalled at the stage with no asset.** The feature deck got the effort; the
  business-case template got none.
- **One asset was deleted outright.** The feature deck served no stage and no role.
- **The stall column says "checked."** The map used win/loss, not intuition.
